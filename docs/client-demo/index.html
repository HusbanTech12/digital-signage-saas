Client progress demo

Digital Menu Board SaaS

A multi-tenant platform to design restaurant menus and push them to in-store screens — managed from one dashboard.

Status today: ~65–70% of MVP complete

The product

What the client is buying

Marketing site

Public landing page that explains the product and drives sign-up.

Operator dashboard

Org, locations, screens, menus, templates, roles — for restaurant teams.

Menu designer

Visual editor to build menu boards and publish to selected screens.

Kiosk display + pairing

TV/tablet client that shows the menu and pairs with a short code.
